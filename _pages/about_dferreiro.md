David Ferreiro is a postdoctoral researcher at the University of A Coruña. His research focuses on the application of artificial intelligence to model and interpret biological interactions at the genomic level.

Building on his background in pathogen evolution at both population and molecular scales, David integrates evolutionary analysis, genomic data and protein structural information to investigate the molecular mechanisms underlying disease evolution. His experience in modelling protein evolution using structural information links genomic patterns to their functional consequences.
