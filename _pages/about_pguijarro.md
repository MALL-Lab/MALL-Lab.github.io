Paula Guijarro Sánchez is a Xunta de Galicia-funded predoctoral researcher at the University of A Coruña, based at INIBIC. Her research focuses on the molecular mechanisms of antimicrobial resistance in multidrug-resistant pathogens, combining genomic and transcriptomic approaches with clinical microbiology. She uses genomic variant analysis to identify novel resistance determinants, and explores technologies such as FTIR and MALDI-TOF MS for the rapid detection of resistance. She completed a research stay at the Philipps-Universität Marburg applying machine learning and genomic encoding methods to antimicrobial resistance research.
