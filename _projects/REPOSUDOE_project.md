The [RePo-SUDOE project](https://interreg-sudoe.eu/en/proyecto-interreg/repo-sudoe/) aims to develop and disseminate technologies for drug repurposing to increase the competitiveness of the SUDOE space in this R&D area, promoting the biopharmaceutical industry and attracting highly trained human resources.

### Main Objectives
* **Scientific Knowledge:** Promote and disseminate drug repurposing technology alongside public and private actors to reach European excellence.
* **Multidisciplinary Collaboration:** Identify innovative opportunities in the SUDOE territory through a transnational network and the creation of a therapeutic target database (using cancer as a primary case study).
* **3D Visualization:** Develop a 3D visualization prototype using Virtual and Augmented Reality for interactive study of biological systems, targeting students and researchers in STEAM areas.

---

### Project Tools & Platforms
The project has developed several specialized platforms to support research and education:

* [**RePo-EDU**](https://repo-edu.citic.udc.es/): Educational resources and training materials.
* [**RePo-VRE**](https://repo-vre.citic.udc.es/): Virtual Research Environment for drug repurposing.
* [**RePo-BOT**](https://repo-bot.citic.udc.es/): Automated assistant for project-related queries and data.

### Consortium Partners
This project brings together, for the first time, partners from different SUDOE regions and various stages of the drug development value chain:

* Instituto Politécnico de Guarda
* **Universidade da Coruña (UDC)**
* Universidade de Santiago de Compostela
* MD.USE Innovations SL
* Sociedade Portuguesa de Saúde Pública
* Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (CNRS)
* Asociación Clúster Saúde de Galicia
